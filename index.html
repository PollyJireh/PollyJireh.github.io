Featured project

Scam Sense: Think Before You Click

Unity 6 · C# · Team capstone

Investigate it. Decide. Learn why.

A first-person 3D educational game that teaches players to recognise phishing messages, suspicious links and fraudulent digital communication. Players explore rooms, read texts and emails, inspect clues, and answer Scam or Legitimate, then get feedback that explains the warning signs.

  • uGUI + TextMeshPro

How I work

A simple process you can follow.

  1. Understand

    We talk through your goals, users and deadline so I build the right thing.

  2. Plan

    I outline the design and scope and agree what "done" looks like.

  3. Develop

    I build in small steps and share progress so you can steer early.

  4. Test and fix

    I check features, devices and edge cases, then fix what breaks.

  5. Deliver

    You get the finished work, the source files and clear next steps.
